Built around how you actually work.

06 / Mahakosh

Patterns across a continent.

Contribute anonymised palm images to the Mahakosh — our shared research pool. Browse patterns from practitioners across India and beyond. Strictly opt-in, per person, reversible anytime.

07 / Voice

Hands full?
Just speak.

Hold the hand, dictate the observation. The mic button transcribes your words into the record — completely hands-free, in the moment.

§ 03 — Privacy by design

Their hands.
Their data.

⌘
Names and contacts — private, always.

Names, phone numbers, dates of birth, face photos, and your personal notes never leave your account. Not even to us.

◐
Mahakosh sharing is opt-in, per person.

Palm images only enter the Mahakosh when you explicitly switch it on for that record. Off by default. Reversible anytime.

∅
Delete everything in one tap.

Settings → Delete Account wipes every record, image, and note permanently. No waiting period. No support ticket.

✕
No ads. No data selling.

Free to download. We don't run ads or sell your data — your records exist to help you, not to be monetised.
